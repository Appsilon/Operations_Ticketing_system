# Deskpro – K.O. screen
1. Products evaluated: **Deskpro Cloud** (Team, Professional, Enterprise). **Deskpro Private** (self-hosted / on-premises) is named only where it changes a verdict (#27). Deskpro Private pricing was not checked.
2. Checked: 2026-11-18
3. Summary: Meets 8 / Partial 8 / Does not meet 0 / Unknown 0 (of 16). Lowest plan meeting the most criteria: **Team** – US$39 / agent / month, minimum 5 agents (billing period not displayed on the pricing page). Every "Meets" except #10 is ticked on Team in the plan comparison. The plan for Approvals (#10) is not stated anywhere checked. Professional (US$59, min. 10 agents) adds Lite Agents and Deskpro AI, including the EU-hosted Berget AI provider (#27).

## Single tool for internal + external?
1. **Deskpro positions one product for both.** Site footer "Solutions": "B2B customer support HR support Internal operations IT help desk". Slack page: "Power internal support across your workspace … IT Help Desk … HR & People Ops … Facilities & Admin" ([Pricing](https://www.deskpro.com/pricing), [Slack](https://www.deskpro.com/product/slack)). Cloud page (quoted from `analysis/market_scan/filter_D.md`, same date): "Every customer or employee request becomes a ticket your team can route, track, and report on." ([Cloud help desk](https://www.deskpro.com/cloud-based-help-desk)).
2. **Desks are separated by department. Clients are grouped as Organizations.** "Departments are the core organizational structure in Deskpro that allow you to control viewing and access permissions for specific tickets." "When a Ticket is created that is assigned to the Support Department, only those members will be able to see the Support tickets" ([KB – two teams](https://support.deskpro.com/en-US/kb/articles/how-do-i-set-up-deskpro-to-have-two-teams-of-agents-who-cant-see-each-others-tickets)). Approvers can be "Agents or CRM Users", so internal managers do not need agent seats ([Approval templates](https://support.deskpro.com/en-US/guides/admin-guide/creating-approval-templates)).
3. **Weak points for one tool:** Slack intake from DMs is "not supported in this release" (#23). No documented per-client agent visibility inside one department (#13). Both are Partial below.
