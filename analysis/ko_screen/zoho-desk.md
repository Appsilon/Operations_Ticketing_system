# Zoho Desk – K.O. screen
1. Products evaluated: **Zoho Desk** (Free, Express, Standard, Professional, Enterprise). Zoho **ServiceDesk Plus (Cloud)** is named only as Zoho's separate internal ITSM/ESM product (see below); it was not evaluated.
2. Checked: 2026-11-18
3. Summary: Meets 8 / Partial 8 / Does not meet 0 / Unknown 0 (of 16). Lowest plan meeting the most criteria: **Enterprise** – US$40 / agent / month billed annually (US$50 billed monthly). It is the lowest plan with multiple ticket layouts per department (#9), Audit Log (#17) and role-based Data Sharing (#13), and it includes 50 free light agents. Professional (US$23 / US$35) meets 8 (#9 drops to Partial).

## Single tool for internal + external?
1. **Zoho positions Desk for both.** FAQ: "Yes, Zoho desk is as effective with customers as it is with internal stakeholders. You can set up multiple departments and have them operate as separate support units with dedicated channels, help centers, SLAs, etc." ([Internal help desk page](https://www.zoho.com/desk/internal-help-desk.html)). Multiple departments start at **Professional** ("Multi-department" listed under Professional; "Multiple department support tracking": Professional 10, Enterprise 50) ([Pricing](https://www.zoho.com/desk/pricing.html), [Plan comparison](https://www.zoho.com/desk/pricing-comparison.html)).
2. **Client side fits; separation per client is the weak point.** Email-to-ticket in all plans (email channels: Professional 10, Enterprise 100). Per-team desks = departments ("Once you assign a department, the agent can access and view only those tickets that belong to that department", [Agents](https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/desk/user-management-and-security/agents-and-teams/articles/add-manage-desk-agents)). For per-client visibility inside the Platform desk, no official per-account rule was found. Options found: one department per client (department add-on on Enterprise: US$7 / department / month monthly, US$6 annually), or Enterprise "Data sharing" by role.
3. **Internal side is weaker on approvals and Slack. Zoho has a separate internal product.** Desk approvals are single requests sent to agents (see #10). Zoho states that the native approval cannot go to non-agents. The Slack app is built for agents (see #23). Zoho's separate ITSM/ESM product is **Zoho ServiceDesk Plus (Cloud)**: "Workflow automation … actions like notifications, field updates, approvals" and "Manage multiple service desk instances with clear data and process segregation for multiple departments, such as facilities, HR and IT" ([ServiceDesk Plus](https://www.zoho.com/online-service-desk-software.html)). Two products are needed only if Desk's approvals (#10) and Slack (#23) gaps are treated as K.O.

**AI add-ons:** no separately priced AI add-on is listed. "Generative AI Services": Express and Standard "BYOK" only; Professional and Enterprise "BYOK & Native". **Licences for approvers and requesters (#29):**
1. **Requesters (employees and clients):** they are contacts / help-center users. Pricing is per agent and no contact fee is listed. No explicit "end users are free" statement was found.
2. **Approvers:** need a full agent licence. Zoho staff (vendor forum, ~2 years old): approval to a non-agent or lite agent is not possible natively. Light agents "cannot … Add, edit or delete records under any module" ([Agents](https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/desk/user-management-and-security/agents-and-teams/articles/add-manage-desk-agents)).
3. **Internal collaborators who only comment:** light agent, US$6 / month (Standard, Professional), 50 free on Enterprise.

## Free trial
Yes. 15 days, any paid plan, no credit card: "activate a 15-day free trial for whichever plan you want to take for a spin … without ever providing a credit card". After the trial: "downgraded to our free plan" ([Pricing FAQ](https://www.zoho.com/desk/pricing.html)).
