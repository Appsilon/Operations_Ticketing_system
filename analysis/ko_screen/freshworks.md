# Freshworks – K.O. screen
1. Products evaluated: **Freshservice** (ITSM, with its Enterprise Service Management layer: workspaces for business teams, the Business Agent add-on licence, and the standalone **Freshservice for Business Teams** plan) and **Freshdesk** (customer service). Each was checked as a single-tool candidate. Freshdesk Omni and Freshservice for MSPs were not evaluated.
2. Checked: 2026-11-18 (all pages fetched 2026-11-18, 13:32–13:47 UTC). No page was blocked.
3. Summary per product:
   - **Freshservice:** Meets 12 / Partial 4 / Does not meet 0 / Unknown 0 (of 16). Lowest plan meeting the most criteria: **Growth, USD 49 per agent/month billed annually (USD 59 billed monthly)**. All 12 "Meets" are available on Growth. Enterprise (price not published) adds only the Audit Log (#17, which stays Partial).
   - **Freshdesk:** Meets 9 / Partial 5 / Does not meet 1 (#10 approvals) / Unknown 1 (#12) (of 16). Lowest plan meeting the most criteria: **Pro, USD 55 per agent/month billed annually (USD 66 billed monthly)**. Enterprise (USD 89/107) adds only the Audit Log (#17, which stays Partial).
   - Plan coverage comes from each pricing page's "Compare features" grid, read by cell position. Help-center articles were used for the detail of each feature.

## Single tool for internal + external?
1. **Freshdesk alone fails the internal side.** It has no ticket approval workflow. The only "Approval workflow" in the Freshdesk plan grid is for knowledge-base content: "Create an approval workflow to review content before it gets published to your knowledge base" (pricing page tooltip, Enterprise only). Its Slack app is built around agents and team channels (#23). On the external side it is strong. Email is converted to tickets, and "Any email sent to your configured email ID will be automatically converted to Freshdesk tickets". Access can be scoped per group: agents "can view tickets assigned to their groups, as well as tickets directly assigned to them". Sources: [Freshdesk pricing](https://www.freshworks.com/freshdesk/pricing/), [Email setup](https://support.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/37541-set-up-your-support-email), [Advanced ticket scope](https://support.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/50000002804-advanced-ticket-scope).
2. **Freshservice covers the internal side and has the building blocks for the external side, but external B2B clients in the same account are not documented.**
   - Internal: separate team desks via workspaces ("a requester can send an email to a workspace’s support address to create a ticket in that workspace"), approvals, and the Slack ServiceBot.
   - Restricted groups give per-team or per-client ticket isolation.
   - Freshservice has two account modes. The workspace features "apply exclusively to accounts in 'Employee Support (ESM)' mode … accounts in 'Managed Services (MSP)' mode … their rules and behavior may differ, and users must switch their account mode". So one account runs either internal (ESM) mode or client-facing (MSP) mode.
   - Not found in any page read: a statement that external B2B clients may be requesters in an ESM-mode account, or terms that forbid it. This is an open question for the vendor (see "Could not verify").
   - Sources: [Multi-workspace email](https://support.freshservice.com/support/solutions/articles/50000011520-requester-friendly-multi-workspace-email-support), [Multiple requester portals](https://support.freshservice.com/support/solutions/articles/50000013959-requester-and-agent-experiences-in-multiple-requester-portal-accounts), [Restricted groups](https://support.freshservice.com/support/solutions/articles/50000003479-restricted-groups).
3. **Combination:** the vendor lists a built-in "Freshservice-Freshdesk Integration" on all four Freshservice plans ([Freshservice pricing](https://www.freshworks.com/freshservice/pricing/), Compare features). If Freshworks confirms that Freshservice (ESM mode) may serve external clients, **Freshservice alone** is the single-tool candidate. If not, only **Freshservice (internal) + Freshdesk (Platform/clients)** covers both. Under decision #2, two tools are considered only if no single tool meets the K.O. criteria.
