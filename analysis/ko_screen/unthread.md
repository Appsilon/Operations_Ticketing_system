3. Summary: Meets 8 / Partial 7 / Does not meet 0 / Unknown 1 (of 16). ## Single tool for internal + external?
1. Yes, one workspace serves both. Projects have a type: "“Internal support” means you’re supporting team members within your organization" and "“External support” means you’re supporting customers outside of your organization". Source: [Projects & Permissions](https://docs.unthread.io/docs/setting-up-your-account/projects-permissions)
2. The portal can be set up for either audience: "You can configure your portal to be for internal support (supporting team members) or external support (supporting external customers)." Homepage: "Capture Slack threads, Teams messages, email, and portal requests without forcing employees or customers into a separate system." Sources: [Customer Portal](https://docs.unthread.io/docs/in-app-support/customer-portal-overview), [unthread.io](https://unthread.io/)
3. The pricing page shows one plan ladder (Pro / Business / Enterprise) with no separate internal and external editions. Caveat: the product depends on Slack. Inbound email is posted to an internal Slack channel ("Unthread allows you to relay support emails to your Slack channel"), and agents answer from Slack or the web inbox. Sources: [Pricing](https://unthread.io/pricing), [Email Support](https://docs.unthread.io/docs/email-support/email-support)
