3. Summary: Meets 9 / Partial 6 / Does not meet 0 / Unknown 1 (of 16). Lowest plan meeting the most criteria: **Engaged** (adds SLA management #15 and multi-level approvals #10, which Essential does not have). Shown price: €86 per agent/month at 30 agents, €101 at 11 agents (excl. tax).

## Single tool for internal + external?
1. Yes, one product. TOPdesk says it handles external support: "are you a service provider offering external support? Make sure you restrict call sharing to users of the same customers or branch." ([Sharing calls](https://docs.topdesk.com/en/sharing-calls-with-other-persons.html)). A TOPdesk customer story (an MSP) says: "Not only will the tool be used internally, but also by their external customers." ([Sword IT Solutions](https://www.topdesk.com/en/customer-stories/sword-it-solutions/))
2. Client email: you can set up a separate mail import for each address. "If your organization receives multiple emails at different addresses, set up different mail imports", each with its own "default values for fields like type, category, and operator". Unknown senders can be turned into new person cards. ([Importing email](https://docs.topdesk.com/en/importing-email.html), [Defining the caller](https://docs.topdesk.com/en/defining-the-caller.html))
3. Separate desks and clients: operator filters limit agents to their own team's tickets: "only view cards linked to their own or to specified operator groups". Branch filters "filters the visible cards by branch". The docs treat a client as a branch: "With other persons from their own branch" ([Operator filters](https://docs.topdesk.com/en/operator-filters.html), [Filters](https://docs.topdesk.com/en/determine-what-operators-may-see-with-filters.html)). I found no dedicated B2B "customer organization" object. In the docs, "external relations" means suppliers.

## Pricing (as displayed on 2026-11-18)
The page shows GBP by default with 30 agents, probably because of where the request came from. Changing the currency (GBP/USD/EUR/CAD) and the agent count (5–50) changes the price per agent. "The more agents you add to TOPdesk, the lower your average price." Prices "do not include taxes". The page says "Per agent/month" but does not state the billing frequency (monthly or annual) or the contract length. The FAQ mentions a "contract term" and says downgrades happen "at the end of your current contract term".
