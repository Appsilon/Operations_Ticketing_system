1. Products evaluated: **Suptask** (Slack-native service desk; plans Starter, Professional, Growth, Custom). Vendor: "Suptask (Happyning AB), 559219-8211, with headquarters at Scheelegatan 28, 112 28 Stockholm, Sweden" ([Privacy Policy](https://www.suptask.com/trust/privacy-policy)).
2. Checked: 2026-11-18
3. Summary: Meets 10 / Partial 6 / Does not meet 0 / Unknown 0 (of 16). Lowest plan meeting the most criteria: **Growth** – $38 / agent / month billed annually ($45 billed monthly). It is the lowest plan with approval sequences (#10) and SLA (#15). Professional ($24 / $29) meets 8: #10 drops to ad-hoc approvals only and #15 is not included.

## Single tool for internal + external?
1. **Yes, the vendor positions one product for both.** Professional plan: "Service desk for internal teams and customer support". Email page: "Email tickets share the same queue as customer support tickets and internal requests." Separate team desks = Inboxes ("One Inbox for each of your team: IT, HR and one for Engineering") ([Pricing](https://www.suptask.com/pricing), [Email ticketing](https://www.suptask.com/ticketing/email), [Inboxes](https://support.suptask.com/setup-and-configure/inboxes/how-to-use-suptask-inboxes)).
2. **Email clients without Slack or a portal are supported from Professional.** "Can I support customers who aren't on Slack? Yes. Customer emails automatically become Slack tickets, and your agents reply from Slack while updates sync back to the customer's inbox as branded email". "Email ticketing is included from the Professional plan … Starter … does not include email." Web Forms (portal-like intake) are Custom-only; not needed, since clients will not use a portal ([Customer support](https://www.suptask.com/products/customer-support-in-slack), [Email ticketing](https://www.suptask.com/ticketing/email), [Pricing](https://www.suptask.com/pricing)).
3. **Caveats.** Agents work only in Slack. Ticket conversations are stored in Slack, not by Suptask: "All messages are stored in Slack - we do not store any replies in our datacenter" ([AI Policy](https://www.suptask.com/trust/ai-policy)). For People requests, the DPA says: "Customer must not submit health data, racial/ethnic data, or other sensitive data without prior agreement" ([DPA](https://www.suptask.com/trust/data-processing-agreement)).
