2. Checked: 2026-11-18
3. Summary: Meets 9 / Partial 6 / Does not meet 0 / Unknown 1 (of 16). Lowest plan meeting the most criteria: **Customer Service Suite Enterprise** ("Suite Enterprise + Copilot", price not published, "Talk to Sales"). It is the only plan that meets #13 (private groups). ## Single tool for internal + external?
1. **Employee Service Suite = a separate instance with its own licence, and contractually internal-only.** The terms say: "Customer may not use its Employee Service instance(s) for external customer support purposes." ([Supplemental terms, "Employee Service" section](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408831944730-Supplemental-terms-Zendesk-s-service-specific-terms)). It is sold "for organizations that want to use Zendesk products exclusively for internal services" ([ES Suite overview](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/8836478757914-Zendesk-Employee-Service-Suite-overview)). The ES and CS plans also have different prices ([ES pricing](https://www.zendesk.com/pricing/employee-service-pricing/) vs [CS pricing](https://www.zendesk.com/pricing/)). In one instance, "all agents must be on the same plan type" ([One or multiple instances](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408883091994-Considering-one-or-multiple-Zendesk-instances)).
2. **Single tool = Customer Service Suite used for both.** Zendesk documents CS accounts serving employees: approvals are listed for "Customer Service Suite Growth, Professional, Enterprise" and are described for both customer and employee service ([Approvals](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/8481179038490-Understanding-approvals-and-how-they-work)). The ES-only extras would be lost: "Workday integration, Service catalog, … Asset management (EAP)" (ES overview).
3. **Separate desks per team or client in one CS account.** Below Enterprise, separation is by group: "In the agent's profile, set Access to Tickets in agent's groups". Zendesk warns this "is not completely secure and the separation can be circumvented. Each admin has access to data from all groups." Full separation needs private groups, which are Enterprise only. Otherwise Zendesk recommends "Two Zendesk accounts … requires purchasing two subscriptions" ([Group separation](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408831313050-How-can-I-prevent-agents-in-one-group-from-seeing-another-group-s-tickets)). Fallback if one CS account is not accepted: **ES Suite (internal) + CS Suite (clients), two accounts and two licences.**

**AI add-ons (listed):**
1. Copilot: USD 50 per agent/month, paid yearly (both pricing pages).
2. AI agents: "pricing based on the successful outcomes they deliver" (billed per "Automated Resolution"). No unit price is displayed.

**Other add-ons (listed):** Workforce Engagement Bundle USD 50 and Contact Center USD 83 (CS page). Quality Assurance USD 35 and Advanced Data Privacy and Protection USD 50 (ES page). All per agent/month, paid yearly.

**Licences for approvers and requesters (#29):**
1. Requesters are end users, and pricing is "seat-based (per agent, per month)".
2. Approvers can be end users: "both agents and end users can still be approvers, with agents responding to requests in the Agent Workspace and end users responding through the help center". However, "End user-approvers must respond to requests from the help center". Approving in Slack is described only for agent approvers ([Approvals](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/8481179038490-Understanding-approvals-and-how-they-work)).
3. Light agents: "Give team members limited access to tickets without paying for more seats". The compare table shows "Up to 100", but which plan column this belongs to could not be read from the page text ([Compare table](https://www.zendesk.com/pricing/#modal.pricing-table)).
4. In one instance, "all agents must be on the same plan type" ([Instances](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408883091994-Considering-one-or-multiple-Zendesk-instances)). Choosing Enterprise for #13 therefore means Enterprise for every agent.

## Free trial
Yes, 14 days. The default is "all features on the Zendesk Customer Service Suite Professional plan", and "Copilot is included by default". An Employee Service Suite Professional trial is available through a separate sign-up link. Trials of other plans are arranged through sales. Source: [Pricing page FAQ](https://www.zendesk.com/pricing/) and [ES pricing FAQ](https://www.zendesk.com/pricing/employee-service-pricing/).
