# Zammad – K.O. screen
1. Products evaluated: Zammad hosted (SaaS) plans Starter v2, Professional v2 and Plus v2. Self-hosted Zammad (with the vendor's support plans Business, Enterprise and Corporation) is noted only where it changes a verdict. Zammad is one product for both internal and external use, with no separate ITSM edition.
2. Checked: 2026-11-18
3. Summary: Meets 6 / Partial 8 / Does not meet 1 / Unknown 1 (of 16). Lowest plan meeting the most criteria: **Plus v2**, €25 per agent/month billed annually or €27 billed monthly, excl. VAT. It adds Core Workflows (#9) and Standard Reporting (#16), which Professional v2 (€16 or €18) does not have. Starter v2 allows at most 5 agents and has no SLAs.

## Single tool for internal + external?
1. Yes, one product. Zammad says the product helps organisations "manage customer inquiries, internal service processes, and cross-team communication in a centralized, transparent, and efficient way" ([press release, 2026-06-18](https://zammad.com/en/company/press/zammad-gmbh-achieves-iso-iec-27001-2022-certification)). It has an "IT Service Desk" use case ([solution page](https://zammad.com/en/solutions/zammad-at-the-it-service-desk), quoted in `analysis/market_scan/filter_C.md`; not re-fetched).
2. B2B clients are modelled as organizations: "often your company’s real client is another company (or organization). Customers can be grouped into organizations" ([user docs: Organizations](https://user-docs.zammad.org/en/latest/extras/organizations.html)). Desks are separated with group permissions: "Use the group permission table to grant per-group privileges" ([admin docs: Group Permissions](https://admin-docs.zammad.org/en/latest/manage/groups/group-permissions.html)).
3. Risk when internal and external tickets share one instance: "Shared Organization" defaults to yes, and the docs warn: "This can cause serious issues if you have e.g. human resources working in the same Zammad instance" ([admin docs: Organizations](https://admin-docs.zammad.org/en/latest/manage/organizations/index.html)).

## Free trial
1. Yes, 30 days: "Start Your 30-Days Trial … You can continue using your existing Zammad instance, including all settings and data" ([getting started](https://zammad.com/en/getting-started)).
2. Plan: "Trial instances are running on a Professional v2 plan with 3 agents. The plan cannot be changed during the trial period". Plus-only features (Core Workflows, Standard Reporting) therefore cannot be tried without contacting sales ([Subscription docs](https://admin-docs.zammad.org/en/latest/system/subscription.html)).
