# HubSpot Service Hub – K.O. screen
1. Products evaluated: HubSpot Service Hub (plans Free / Starter / Professional / Enterprise), whose ticketing workspace is called "Help Desk". I also checked the HubSpot Slack integration and HubSpot platform pages on seats, audit log, security and hosting. The company already uses HubSpot for sales and marketing. Which hubs and tiers it has is **not known**, and I did not assume any.
2. Checked: 2026-11-18. I fetched 30 official HubSpot pages with `tools/pw_text.js`, and no page was blocked. Two URLs redirected: `legal.hubspot.com/hubspot-regional-data-hosting-policy` went to the Customer Terms of Service, and `knowledge.hubspot.com/object-settings/automate-ticket-pipelines` went to "Set up pipeline automations for objects".
3. Summary: Meets 8 / Partial 6 / Does not meet 1 (#10 approvals) / Unknown 1 (of 16). Lowest plan meeting the most criteria: **Service Hub Professional**. The Help Desk workspace, SLAs, ticket workflows, the form channel, the support portal, Slack ticket sync and the CRM-object audit log all start at Professional, and Enterprise adds no further K.O. criterion. Price: Service Seat **$100/month per seat** (Products & Services Catalog). The pricing page shows "Starts at $90/mo/seat $100/mo/seat" (USD). A required one-time "Professional Onboarding" fee of $1,500 is added.

## Single tool for internal + external?
1. **Internal requests (IT/People/Finance/Operations): no vendor evidence either way.** None of the official pages I checked positions Service Hub for employee requests. The Help Desk docs are written for customers: "Channels are the ways customers communicate with your team" ([channels](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/help-desk/connect-channels-to-help-desk)). The mechanism is generic, though: "Team email: Connect a shared email address to automatically create tickets from incoming emails". Several team addresses (for example it@ or people@) could therefore feed one Help Desk. *That reading is mine, not a vendor claim.* Two gaps hit internal use most: there are no ticket approvals (#10 Does not meet), and Slack is not a requester channel (#23 Partial).
2. **External clients:** this is the product's core use. Email-first client support works with no portal required (#1 Meets), and a support portal is optional (#12). Separation per client is weak: access follows the **ticket owner's team**, not the client (#13 Partial).
3. **One product or two:** Service Hub Professional handles both kinds of request in one Help Desk, and no second HubSpot product is needed for the core flows. No HubSpot hub offers ticket approvals. Approvals exist for deals only, in Sales Hub Enterprise ([approvals overview](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/account-management/overview-of-approvals-in-hubspot)). Because HubSpot is already in use, Service Hub would probably be added to the existing account (*my assumption*). The data centre (#27) and the Core Seat price ("all your Core Seats … will be priced based on the highest level of your subscription services", [catalog](https://legal.hubspot.com/hubspot-product-and-services-catalog)) would then depend on that unknown account.

**AI add-ons (HubSpot Credits):**
1. Included credits each month: Starter 500, Professional 3,000, Enterprise 5,000. "Unused credits do not roll over".
2. Additional credits: "$0.010 per credit" Pay-as-You-Go ("invoiced in increments of 10 credits"), or a "Capacity pack $10 USD per additional capacity pack of 1,000 credits" per month.
3. Customer agent: "Resolve one conversation (text-based channels) 50" credits. At the additional-credit rate that is $0.50 per resolved conversation (my calculation).
4. Promotion: "use Customer Agent free for 14 days" when buying "at least 1 Service Professional or Enterprise Seat".
5. Sources: https://legal.hubspot.com/hubspot-product-and-services-catalog ; https://www.hubspot.com/pricing/service

**Licences for requesters and approvers (#29):**
1. Requesters, internal and external: they are CRM contacts, not users, and seats are assigned per user ("Every user … is assigned a seat"). Portal access is granted to "contacts" via access groups. Email requesters need no account at all. I found no vendor statement that contacts are never billed in Service Hub.
2. Approvers: Service Hub has no ticket approvals (#10). If a manager has to act in HubSpot, a free View-Only Seat "can't save reports, edit records…", although it can "Log notes on CRM records they can view". Any edit needs at least a paid Core Seat ($20 / $50 / $75 per month for Starter / Professional / Enterprise, per the catalog). The Core Seat price follows the highest tier across all hubs already owned.
3. Slack requesters: it is not documented whether "Create a ticket" in Slack works for Slack users without a HubSpot user.

## Free trial
