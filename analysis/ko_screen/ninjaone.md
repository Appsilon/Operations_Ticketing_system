# NinjaOne Ticketing – K.O. screen
1. Products evaluated: **NinjaOne Ticketing** (NinjaOne, LLC), a module of the NinjaOne platform. It is not sold as a separate edition with its own plans. It is switched on inside the platform: "Navigate to Administration, then Apps, then Installed. Locate Ticketing and click the Disabled label to change the status to Enabled" ([Ticket Setup](https://www.ninjaone.com/docs/ticketing/ticket-setup/)). Product page: "Ticketing runs on the same platform as your endpoints, asset information, and process documentation. No add-on, no separate login. It's all just NinjaOne" ([Ticketing](https://www.ninjaone.com/ticketing/)). No official page found that says whether Ticketing needs other NinjaOne modules (RMM / endpoint licences) or can be bought alone. NinjaOne also lists a separate "PSA" product in the site footer; it was not evaluated. Several docs show "Already a NinjaOne customer? Log in to view more guides", so some guides are login-only.
2. Checked: 2026-11-18
3. Summary: Meets 7 / Partial 8 / Does not meet 0 / Unknown 1 (of 16). Lowest plan meeting the most criteria: no public plans; one platform, quote only. Published range: **$3.75 USD per device (endpoint) per month at 50 or fewer endpoints**, down to $1.50 at 10,000 endpoints. Price is per managed device, not per agent; whether Ticketing is included in that price is not stated.

## Single tool for internal + external?
1. **Yes, according to the vendor, but IT-focused.** FAQ: "Is NinjaOne Ticketing suitable for internal IT teams or MSPs? Both. Internal IT teams use it to move off email chains and shared inboxes, while MSPs use it to manage client support requests natively alongside their existing NinjaOne environment" ([Ticketing, FAQ](https://www.ninjaone.com/ticketing/)). External clients are "Contacts": "known users who can submit tickets via email" ([Ticket Setup](https://www.ninjaone.com/docs/ticketing/ticket-setup/)). Each client can be an "organization" with its own default SLA ([SLA doc](https://www.ninjaone.com/docs/ticketing/ninjaone-ticketing-getting-started/service-level-agreements-sla/)).
2. **Built around devices.** "Every ticket opens with device data, asset history, and remediation tools already in view" ([Ticketing](https://www.ninjaone.com/ticketing/)). No official page found that describes use by HR/People, Finance or Operations desks.
3. **Internal desks are not separated inside one organization.** "a technician with access to an organization will have access to all tickets for that organization, regardless of the permissions set in the Ticketing section" ([Technician and User Permissions](https://www.ninjaone.com/docs/ticketing/ticketing-technician-user-permissions/)). Per-client separation works through organizations. Separating IT, People and Finance inside one company organization is not documented (see #13). Licensing is per device ([Pricing](https://www.ninjaone.com/pricing/)). How a team that mainly answers client email would be licensed is not stated (see "Could not verify").
