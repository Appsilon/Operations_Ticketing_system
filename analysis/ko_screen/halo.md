## Single tool for internal + external?
1. **Yes, as one product or plan, based on vendor statements: one licence covers internal and external use.** Pricing FAQ: "There are no feature tiers and no separate editions for IT, service, sales or projects". The same price covers "IT, customer service, sales, projects, marketing" ([Pricing](https://www.usehalo.com/pricing)). FAQ: "Every department operates within the same platform and the same licence, with no additional cost for extending service management beyond IT" ([Halo AI page, FAQ](https://www.usehalo.com/halo-ai)). The included feature list has internal items ("HR Service Delivery", "Finance & Legal", "Employee Portal", "Approval Workflows", "Multi-Dept. Routing") and external ones ("Omnichannel Inbox", "Shared Mailboxes", "Self-Service Portal") ([Pricing](https://www.usehalo.com/pricing)). **So HaloITSM vs HaloPSA is no longer a choice between two products. Two products are not needed.**
2. **Client email plus separate desks per team and per client are documented.**
   1. Each mailbox sets its own ticket type: "The type of ticket (ticket type) that is logged from the email will be determined by the mailbox the email was sent into" ([guide 900](https://www.usehalo.com/guides/900)).
   2. Agents see their teams' tickets: "Can view Tickets that are assigned to other Agents - Similarly can view tickets for other agents in their teams" ([guide 883](https://www.usehalo.com/guides/883)).
   3. Per-client restriction: "we will set it so that the agent only has access to their associated client's information" ([guide 1194](https://www.usehalo.com/guides/1194)).
   4. Portal per group: "The portal can be configured separately for different departments or user groups" ([Halo AI page, FAQ](https://www.usehalo.com/halo-ai)).
3. **Open point (not verified):** the trial form asks for one use case ("Business Teams & Internal IT (ESM & ITSM)", "Customer Service (CSM)", "Managed Services (PSA)"…) ([Pricing](https://www.usehalo.com/pricing), trial dialog). No source says whether this choice only sets a starting template or limits the instance. To ask: see "Could not verify" #4.
