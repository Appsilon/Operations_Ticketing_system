# OTRS – K.O. screen
1. Products evaluated: OTRS (vendor EasyVista-OTRS GmbH), vendor-managed SaaS ("OTRS Managed"), packages Starter / Advanced / Pro. On-premises is noted only where it changes a verdict. The ((OTRS)) Community Edition was not evaluated. Feature evidence comes mainly from the official manuals: OTRS Administration Manual Release 2025.6.1 (14 Jul 2025, "ADMIN") and OTRS User Manual Release 2025.8.1 (11 Nov 2026, "USER"), both PDFs on academy.otrs.com.
2. Checked: 2026-11-18
3. Summary: Meets 5 / Partial 10 / Does not meet 0 / Unknown 1 (of 16). Lowest plan meeting the most criteria: **Starter**. Price not public ("Get a quote"). Advanced is needed for Web Services / REST API, which is the only route to Slack (#23), and it includes AI. Flag: #26 is Partial only because of verdict rule 8. OTRS states it holds neither ISO 27001 nor SOC 2, and only its data-centre operators are certified.

## Single tool for internal + external?
1. Yes, one product for both. "OTRS is a service management solution that can be used by any department in your company." and "Both external and internal customers are quickly and continuously informed about the status of their request." ([OTRS product page](https://otrs.com/otrs-software-solutions/otrs/)). The vendor lists Help Desk, Customer Service & Support, HR Management and Office Management as solutions: "Choose the solution that fits your use case or combine them on one platform." ([AI page, solutions block](https://otrs.com/otrs-software-solutions/artificial-intelligence/))
2. External clients: "Customers submit incident or repair requests via the customer portal or supported communication channels" ([Customer Service & Support](https://otrs.com/otrs-software-solutions/customer-service-support/)). The external interface has a per-client view: "The view in this section shows all tickets assigned to a certain customer." (USER §2.6.3)
3. Separation by team: "Each team can have its physical email mailbox." (ADMIN §3.9). Queues are tied to permission groups: "The group creates a permission link between the queue and an agent or a customer user." (ADMIN §2.7.2). Gap: there is no Slack intake for employees (#23).

Pricing is configured in three steps: licensed users, then package, then extras. No numbers are published. Overage: "If the number of users agreed with the customer is exceeded, OTRS AG reserves the right to issue a subsequent invoice." The GTC on the EasyVista licensing page are those of OTRS AG (2024). The current entity is EasyVista-OTRS GmbH, so these terms may be outdated.

AI add-on: "AI functionalities are automatically included in the OTRS service contracts Advanced and Pro." For Starter, AI is "available through a flexible credit based model called AI Credits". Credits are monthly and "not carried over automatically". No price for AI Credits is listed. "Currently, detailed usage visibility is not yet available." ([AI page FAQ](https://otrs.com/otrs-software-solutions/artificial-intelligence/))

Licences for requesters and approvers (#29): Unknown. Pricing counts "licensed users", and the GTC say "contractually agreed number of users"; neither says whether customer users are free. The product separates agents from customer users: approval dialogs can be "Available in … External Interface" (ADMIN §5.4.2). No official page says whether such customer-user approvers need a licence.

## Free trial
Yes, on request: "Want to try OTRS for free? Let us know, and we'll set up a trial instance for you." ([On-Premise & Managed](https://otrs.com/otrs-software-solutions/otrs/otrs-on-premise-managed/)). The length is not stated; the terms only say it is "free of charge and limited in a timely manner". "After expiration of the Trial the OTRS instance and all data is deleted and cannot be restored." ([Terms of Service Free Trial](https://otrs.com/terms-of-service/)). The plan used for the trial is not stated.
