1. Products evaluated: Atlassian Service Collection (cloud): Jira Service Management (JSM), Customer Service Management (CSM), Assets and Rovo. Plans Free / Standard / Premium / Enterprise ($0 / $26.90 / $61.60 per agent/month at 11 agents (assumption), monthly, $ as displayed; Enterprise via sales). Verdicts are for JSM unless a row says CSM. Plans, K.O. results and trial: see `analysis/ko_screen/atlassian.md`.
2. Checked: 2026-11-18

Notes on method:
1. Pages were fetched with headless Chromium (`tools/pw_text.js`; the pricing page also with a scratch variant that reads hidden table text). 43 page loads. No page was blocked. All evidence comes from official pages: atlassian.com, support.atlassian.com, developer.atlassian.com, marketplace.atlassian.com, and for integrations zapier.com and getoutline.com. No secondary source is used as evidence.
2. "Lowest plan" comes from the Service Collection pricing comparison table (https://www.atlassian.com/software/jira/service-management/pricing) when it lists the feature. Help pages often state no plan; then the cell says "Not stated". Free is capped at 3 agents.
3. Rovo features (#18, #19, #20, #22) are not shown in the Free column of the pricing table; Standard is the lowest plan that lists "Rovo Agents".
4. The web-search quota ran out near the end. Items that would have needed more URL discovery (#35 GitHub/Notion links, #47 native reminders, #55 calendar, #57 data export) were scored on the pages already fetched.
