# HubSpot Service Hub – full screen (requirements outside the K.O. set)
1. Products evaluated: HubSpot Service Hub (plans Free / Starter / Professional / Enterprise) with its ticketing workspace "Help Desk". I also checked HubSpot platform features that Service Hub uses (workflows, forms, properties, imports, SSO, privacy tools, mobile app), the HubSpot MCP server and Claude connector, HubSpot-built marketplace apps (Notion Data Sync), and the other side's official pages for Zapier and Productive. The company's existing HubSpot hubs and tiers are **not known**, and I assumed none.
2. Checked: 2026-11-18. I made 38 page fetches with `tools/pw_text.js` and reused 30 official pages that the K.O. screen fetched earlier the same day. No page was blocked. Fetches that returned nothing useful: one guessed URL returned 404 (`knowledge.hubspot.com/help-desk/connect-a-team-email-channel-to-help-desk`; the correct page is `connect-a-team-channel-to-help-desk`), `zapier.com/apps/aboard/integrations` returned 404, and four HubSpot Marketplace search URLs (`ecosystem.hubspot.com/marketplace/apps?query=...`) ignored the query and showed the marketplace home page. WebSearch was used only to find URLs, and its budget ran out before the last search.
3. Summary: Meets 17 / Partial 23 / Does not meet 0 / Unknown 3 (of 43). Must have (13 of the 43: #2, #7, #8, #11, #14, #18, #19, #20, #21, #22, #24, #25, #28): Meets 7 / Partial 6 / Does not meet 0 / Unknown 0.

## Add-on costs found
1. HubSpot Credits (AI and automation): included each month are Starter 500, Professional 3,000 and Enterprise 5,000. Extra credits cost "$0.010 per credit" Pay-as-You-Go (invoiced in increments of 10 credits), or a "Capacity pack $10 USD per additional capacity pack of 1,000 credits" per month, in USD. Source: https://legal.hubspot.com/hubspot-product-and-services-catalog
2. Customer agent (#30): "Resolve one conversation (text-based channels) 50" credits. At the extra-credit rate that is $0.50 per resolved conversation (my calculation). Voice (Beta) costs 50 credits per minute. Source: catalog (as above).
3. Knowledge Base Agent (#20, Beta): "Generate one new knowledge base article 200" credits, which is $2.00 per article at the extra-credit rate (my calculation). Accepting a suggested edit uses no credits. Sources: catalog ; https://knowledge.hubspot.com/knowledge-base/create-a-knowledge-base-agent
4. AI workflow actions: "Execute one AI action in a workflow 10" credits. Data agent: "Generate response to one prompt for one record 10" credits. Source: catalog.
5. Reply recommendations (#18): no credits ("Using reply recommendations does not consume HubSpot credits"), but a Service Seat is required: Professional $100 or Enterprise $150 per seat per month, USD (catalog, from the K.O. screen). Source: https://knowledge.hubspot.com/help-desk/use-reply-recommendations-in-help-desk
6. AI Category and Language (#19): require Service Hub Enterprise ($150 per seat per month plus a one-time $3,500 Enterprise Onboarding, per the K.O. screen). The catalog rate sheet lists no credit rate for them. Source: https://knowledge.hubspot.com/properties/hubspots-default-ticket-properties
7. SSO (#25): included in any Professional or Enterprise hub. I found no separate SSO subscription.
8. Notion Data Sync (#22, #35): the app is listed at "$0" but needs a "paid subscription to Data Hub Starter" (price not checked) and a Notion subscription. Source: https://ecosystem.hubspot.com/marketplace/listing/notion-data-sync
9. "Send a webhook" and "Custom code" workflow actions: Data Hub Professional or Enterprise only (price not checked). Source: https://knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/choose-your-workflow-actions
10. HubSpot connector for Claude (#21): free on the HubSpot side, but it needs "a paid Anthropic subscription (Pro, Max, Team, or Enterprise)". Source: https://www.hubspot.com/claude/connector
11. Productive integration (#53): needs Productive's "Ultimate subscription plan" (price not checked). Source: https://help.productive.io/en/articles/6877575-hubspot-integration
12. Zapier (#50, #51, #54): a Zapier subscription may be needed beyond the free tier (price not checked).
