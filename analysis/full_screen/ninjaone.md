# NinjaOne Ticketing – full screen (requirements outside the K.O. set)
1. Products evaluated: NinjaOne Ticketing (https://www.ninjaone.com/ticketing/), an app inside the NinjaOne platform ("Ticketing runs on the same platform as your endpoints, asset information, and process documentation. No add-on, no separate login."). Features from other NinjaOne modules are named per row: NinjaOne Documentation (knowledge base, checklists), Endpoint Management / RMM agent (conditions, scheduled scripts, NinjaOne Chat), Katana AI (early access), NinjaOne MCP server. Pricing is quote-based and per device: "NinjaOne starts as low as $1.50 USD per month at 10,000 endpoints, increasing to $3.75 USD at 50 or fewer endpoints; pricing varies by region and products purchased." Free trial: "Yes, we offer a 14-day free trial." (https://www.ninjaone.com/pricing/, FAQ). No public plans or tiers; no separate price for Ticketing per technician was found.
2. Checked: 2026-11-18
3. Summary: Meets 9 / Partial 24 / Does not meet 2 / Unknown 8 (of 43). Must have (13 of the 43: #2, #7, #8, #11, #14, #18, #19, #20, #21, #22, #24, #25, #28): Meets 4 / Partial 6 / Does not meet 0 / Unknown 3.

Notes on method:
1. "Lowest plan": NinjaOne publishes no plans, so every row says "Not stated". Where a feature sits in another NinjaOne module, the row names the module.
2. Many docs pages show "Already a NinjaOne customer? Log in to view more guides". The guide "Katana AI: Getting Started with the Model Context Protocol" (https://www.ninjaone.com/docs/ticketing/katana-ai-getting-started-with-the-model-context-protocol/) failed with ERR_TOO_MANY_REDIRECTS (treated as blocked; not circumvented).
3. https://zapier.com/apps/ninjaone/integrations returned 404. A WebSearch on zapier.com found no NinjaOne app (only "Ninja Forms", a different product). Zapier is also absent from the NinjaOne integrations page.
4. FAQ accordions on ninjaone.com are collapsed; their text was read from the page DOM (textContent), not by clicking anything.
5. WebSearch was used only to find URLs (6 searches). No secondary source is used for any verdict.
