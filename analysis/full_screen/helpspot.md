# HelpSpot – full screen (requirements outside the K.O. set)
1. Products evaluated: **HelpSpot** (UserScape, Inc.), product docs for version 5.8.x. HelpSpot has **one plan**: "Every feature at every tier. No add-ons", priced by agent tier and "SOLD ANNUALLY". Pricing page calculator: "$ 2,199 /year Just $18.33 per agent, per month" for 10 agents (USD). Vendor's sample tiers (llms-full.txt, "subject to change"): 3 agents $899/year, 5 agents $1,299/year, 10 agents $2,199/year. Same price for US Cloud (AWS US-East), EU Cloud (AWS Frankfurt) and Self-Hosted. Trial: "START 21-DAY FREE TRIAL … NO CREDIT CARD REQUIRED". Evaluated as vendor-hosted **EU Cloud**; self-hosting is noted in a row only where it changes the picture (#28, #31, #45, #58). "Lowest plan" = "Single plan" throughout (all tiers, from 3 agents).
2. Checked: 2026-11-18. Pages were fetched 17:21–17:30 UTC with Playwright/Chromium through the session proxy (27 page loads; four of them are the printer-friendly versions of the whole Admin Manual, Users Manual, API Manual and FAQ on support.helpspot.com, and section links below point to the matching kb.page). `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt` are vendor-written summaries on helpspot.com (official, marketing-style); they are cited only where the docs say nothing more specific. **Blocked:** none. **404:** Zapier pair pages `helpspot/integrations/notion`, `helpspot/integrations/hubspot` and `helpspot/integrations/google-calendar` (a 404 does not prove that no Zap is possible). **Not checked:** the other product's side for Notion, Outline, HubSpot, Productive, Aboard, ServiceNow and Google Calendar. WebSearch was not used. No secondary sources were used.
3. Summary: Meets 17 / Partial 25 / Does not meet 1 / Unknown 0 (of 43). Must have (13 of the 43: #2, #7, #8, #11, #14, #18, #19, #20, #21, #22, #24, #25, #28): Meets 6 / Partial 7 / Does not meet 0 / Unknown 0.

## Add-on costs found
1. None for HelpSpot features. Pricing page: "Every feature at every tier. No add-ons, no "enterprise only" limitations." Checked 2026-11-18, https://www.helpspot.com/pricing.
2. AI: included. "AI included. No add-ons. No separate API key. No extra fees." and "Standard AI usage for your ticket volume is included." What happens above "standard" usage is not stated. https://www.helpspot.com/platform/ai.

4. Onboarding: "Free Professional Onboarding". https://www.helpspot.com/pricing.
5. Third-party costs, not priced here: a Zapier subscription (needed for #22 and #41–#56 routes and for Slack), and Formstack (a separate product) for a no-code form route.
6. EU Cloud: "EU hosting is the same price as US Cloud and Self-Hosted." https://www.helpspot.com/hosting/eu-cloud.
