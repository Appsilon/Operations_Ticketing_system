# Halo – full screen (requirements outside the K.O. set)
1. Products evaluated: Halo (HaloITSM and HaloPSA are one platform with one licence on usehalo.com). Single plan, "The all-in-one for everyone": £69 per agent per month, billed annually (GBP), plus a required onboarding package at a bespoke price (https://www.usehalo.com/pricing). Plan, K.O. results and trial: see `analysis/ko_screen/halo.md`.
2. Checked: 2026-11-18
3. Summary: Meets 24 / Partial 18 / Does not meet 0 / Unknown 1 (of 43). Must have (13 of the 43: #2, #7, #8, #11, #14, #18, #19, #20, #21, #22, #24, #25, #28): Meets 10 / Partial 2 / Does not meet 0 / Unknown 1.

Notes on method:
1. "Lowest plan": Halo has one plan. Pricing FAQ: "Halo has no tiers, editions or add-ons. All current and future capabilities like automation, AI, CMDB, project management and reporting are included as standard for every agent" and "every feature, module and integration included in that one price" (https://www.usehalo.com/pricing). So every row says "Single plan".
2. The docs are one shared library (`https://www.usehalo.com/guides/<id>`). Some guides are written for HaloPSA (e.g. guide 2370 "Introduction to HaloPSA"). They are used here because the platform and licence are the same (K.O. screen, point 1).
3. Quotes from guide 2382 (SLA warnings) and guide 1194 were taken from the K.O. screen (same date). They were not re-fetched.
4. Pages that returned 404 on 2026-11-18: guides 981 (Statuses), 964 (Service Catalogue), 1209 (Multiple portals with different branding) on usehalo.com; `halopsa.com/guides/article?kbid=1209`; `zapier.com/apps/halo-service-solutions/integrations/notion`. Guide 1933 (Ticket Views) loaded with no content. No page was blocked.
5. The integrations catalogue (https://www.usehalo.com/integrations) rendered "Results: 100" but only entries A–F were visible in the fetched text. "No native integration found" therefore means: not in the visible part of the catalogue and not in the guides read. It is not proof of absence.
6. WebSearch was used only to find URLs. No secondary source is used for any verdict. The session's WebSearch budget ran out at the end of the check.

## Add-on costs found
1. AI: **no Halo add-on.** "no separate AI licence" ([Halo AI](https://www.usehalo.com/halo-ai)). The Default Halo Connection is "Free to use" ([Guide 2385](https://www.usehalo.com/guides/2385)).
2. AI with an own connection (optional, paid to a third party, not to Halo): Azure OpenAI "Tokens will need to be purchased to use Azure OpenAI, pricing depends on the models you choose. Requires an Azure subscription". The OpenAI and Amazon Bedrock options are also paid per model. Own connections also need "an additional vector search database". No price is listed ([Guide 2385](https://www.usehalo.com/guides/2385)).
3. Integrations and SSO: included. "every feature, module and integration included in that one price" ([Pricing](https://www.usehalo.com/pricing)).
4. Onboarding: "*Onboarding packages are required with purchase at a bespoke cost depending on scope". No figure ([Pricing](https://www.usehalo.com/pricing)).
5. Bespoke reports from Halo: "large bespoke reports may incur a fee for the work to be done". No figure ([Guide 887](https://www.usehalo.com/guides/887)).
6. Zapier routes (#43, #50, possibly #53): a Zapier subscription is separate. The Zapier page shows "Free tier available". Paid tiers were not checked ([Zapier app](https://zapier.com/apps/halo-service-solutions/integrations)).
