## Add-on costs found
1. AI: no separate AI add-on. AI comes bundled by plan. Engaged: AI incident categorization, AI knowledge item generation, AI search. Excellent adds the answer, writing and translation assistants, summarization, AI triage, TOPdesk Robin and the MCP server. My calculation from listed prices: Engaged to Excellent adds €42 per agent/month at 11 agents (€101 to €143, EUR, excl. tax, billing frequency not stated). Source: https://www.topdesk.com/en/pricing/?pa=11&pv=eur ; https://docs.topdesk.com/en/ai-features.html
2. Ebbot virtual support agent: "Add-on for Engaged plan" and "Add-on for Excellent plan"; no price shown. Source: pricing page above.
3. Live chat: "Add-on for Engaged plan". The pricing table contradicts itself for Excellent: the Omnichannel section says "Included in Excellent plan", the Self-service section says "Add-on for Excellent plan". No price shown. Source: pricing page above.
4. End-user channels ("Integrate with end user tools like WhatsApp"): add-on on all plans; no price shown. Source: pricing page above.
5. SSO: included in all plans ("Single Sign On Included in Essential plan"); no separate subscription. Source: pricing page above.
6. Custom call import (#41): custom imports are built by "TOPdesk Consultancy or Support". The FAQ says "additional services such as implementation … do come at a surcharge". No price shown. Source: https://docs.topdesk.com/en/importing-data-into-topdesk.html ; pricing page above.
7. Marketplace apps: the marketplace filter shows "Price free(39) paid(94)". The price of "Link TOPdesk => ServiceNow" was not checked. Source: https://marketplace.topdesk.com/?s=servicenow&post_type=product
