1. Plans on the pricing page on 2026-11-18: **Pro "Starting at $85 per agent / month" ("Minimum 5 seats · Conversation limits apply")**, **Business** and **Enterprise** ("Get in touch", no price shown). "All plans include a 14-day free trial". The page has an Annual/Monthly toggle. ## Add-on costs found
1. No separately priced add-ons were found. Business and Enterprise prices are not published ("Get in touch"). Only Pro has a price: USD 85/agent/month, "Minimum 5 seats · Conversation limits apply". Whether the price is annual or monthly is not confirmed. Source: https://unthread.io/pricing (2026-11-18).
2. These features need a higher plan (price unknown):
   1. Business: SSO, HRIS integration & directory sync, AI automation builder, self-learning documentation, custom RBAC.
   2. Enterprise: data residency, dedicated hosting, Audit Logs (also stated on https://docs.unthread.io/docs/analytics/audit-logs).

   Source: https://unthread.io/pricing.
3. AI: no AI add-on price is listed. Bring-your-own model is for "customers on higher plans" (plan not named). With Claude via Google Vertex, "Your Google Cloud account manages model access, quota, and inference charges", so model costs go to your own cloud bill. Sources: https://docs.unthread.io/docs/unthread-ai/introduction-to-unthread-ai ; https://docs.unthread.io/docs/unthread-ai/connect-anthropic-claude-through-google-vertex-ai. The audit log lists "AI usage limits" as a tenant setting, but no limits were found.
