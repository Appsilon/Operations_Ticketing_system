# Freshservice – full screen (requirements outside the K.O. set)
1. Products evaluated: Freshservice (Freshworks), plans Starter / Growth / Pro / Enterprise (USD 19 / 49 / 99 / "Custom" per agent/month billed annually; USD 29 / 59 / 119 monthly), including the Enterprise Service Management layer (workspaces for business teams, Business Agent licence USD 49/agent/month on Pro and Enterprise). Freshservice for Business Teams is noted only where an article says it applies. Freshdesk is not evaluated here. Plans, K.O. results and trial: see `analysis/ko_screen/freshworks.md`.
2. Checked: 2026-11-18
3. Summary: Meets 22 / Partial 19 / Does not meet 0 / Unknown 2 (of 43). Must have (13 of the 43: #2, #7, #8, #11, #14, #18, #19, #20, #21, #22, #24, #25, #28): Meets 9 / Partial 4 / Does not meet 0 / Unknown 0.

Notes on method:
1. Plan gating comes from the "Compare features" grid of the [pricing page](https://www.freshworks.com/freshservice/pricing/). The grid is collapsed in the rendered text, so it was read from the page's embedded page data (`__NEXT_DATA__`, same page, same fetch, 2026-11-18). Add-on prices come from the same data.
2. Many help-center articles carry the note "If certain options do not appear in your account, they may not be included in your current plan" without naming a plan. In those cases the cell says "Not stated".
3. Quotes for #40 (restricted groups), #47 (time-based SLA rule) and #49 (reporting-manager approvals) were taken from the K.O. screen (same date). They were not re-fetched.
4. The K.O. open question still applies to every client-facing row (#2, #32, #52, #54): one account runs in either "Employee Support (ESM)" or "Managed Services (MSP)" mode. That external B2B clients may be requesters in an ESM account is not verified.
5. About 45 page fetches. No page was blocked. Two URLs returned 404 (noted in the rows). The shared web-search budget ran out near the end, so #31, #47 and #48 rest on pages already fetched.

## Add-on costs found
Prices are from the [pricing page](https://www.freshworks.com/freshservice/pricing/) data, checked 2026-11-18. USD is shown to this session. EUR values are in the same page data.
1. **Freddy AI Copilot** (needed for #18, #19, #20, and for solution hints in #44): USD 29/agent/month billed annually, USD 35 billed monthly (EUR 29 / 35). Offered on Pro and Enterprise only; the grid shows it unavailable on Starter and Growth. Enterprise also lists it under "AI Add-ons", so it is not included there.
2. **Freddy AI Agent (Classic)** (#30): Enterprise only. "Each Freshservice Enterprise license includes 1,200 sessions of Freddy AI Agent (Classic) per year". No price for extra sessions is listed on the pricing page.
3. **MCP actions** (#21): included per account per year (Growth 1,200, Pro 6,000, Enterprise 12,000). "Additional actions available at" USD 15 (EUR 13) per pack of 1,000.
4. **Connector App Tasks** (HubSpot #52, ServiceNow #54): Pro and Enterprise. "Includes one-time free credit of 500 tasks, with additional packs at" USD 80 (EUR 80) "per 5000 tasks. Valid until the end of the billing cycle."
5. **Orchestration transactions** (#43): included per account per month (Starter 1,000, Growth 2,000, Pro 5,000, Enterprise 20,000). Extra packs cost USD 250 (EUR 215) per 1,000 transactions, "Valid until the end of the billing cycle."
6. **Business Agent License** (People, Finance and Operations agents): USD 49 (EUR 49)/agent/month, Pro and Enterprise only.
7. **Day passes** (occasional agents): "Freshservice offers 3 free Day Passes at the time of sign up … you can purchase Day Passes starting from $3."
8. **Other:** E-signature USD 90 per pack of 30 credits (Pro, Enterprise). Project Management licences USD 15/user/month (Pro, Enterprise).
9. **SSO:** no separate subscription; the grid lists Single Sign-On on all plans.
10. **On-call notifications:** no extra charge ("You won't be charged additionally for the notifications", [On-call](https://support.freshservice.com/support/solutions/articles/50000003777-learn-all-about-freshservice-on-call-management)).
