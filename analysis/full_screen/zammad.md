## Add-on costs found
1. Zammad AI (managed AI provider): €0.03 per AI call ("an interaction with an AI feature, e.g., generating of a ticket summary"). The AI features are available on Professional v2 and Plus v2. Calls are bought as prepaid credits (400 to 500,000 per one-time or automatic re-charge), and 500 calls are free for testing. One AI agent run or one summary is one call. A bring-your-own provider (Anthropic, OpenAI, Mistral and others) is billed by that provider. The FAQ ties the €0.03 fee to "When using Zammad AI".
