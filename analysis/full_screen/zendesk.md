# Zendesk Customer Service Suite – full screen (requirements outside the K.O. set)
1. Products evaluated: **Zendesk Customer Service Suite** with the plans on the CS pricing page on 2026-11-18: **Support Team (USD 19), Suite Team (USD 55), Suite Professional (USD 115), Suite Enterprise + Copilot (price not published)**, all per agent/month, paid yearly (see `analysis/ko_screen/zendesk.md`). Zendesk Employee Service Suite was not evaluated, because its terms forbid external customer support. Many help-center plan lines name **"Suite Growth"**, which is **not shown on the CS pricing page**. In that case the "Lowest plan" column says "Suite Growth (HC)", and the lowest plan with a published price is Suite Professional.
2. Checked: 2026-11-18. Pages were fetched 15:10–15:21 UTC with `tools/pw_text.js` (53 page loads). **Blocked:** `www.zendesk.com/marketplace/...` returned 403 "Just a moment..." (Cloudflare), so Marketplace listings were not read. **Sign-in required:** two help articles (4408846853274 "Managing security settings in Admin Center" and 4408887441690 "Can I bulk create tickets to email my users?"). **404:** help article 9544621220762 (service catalog GA announcement), plus the Zapier pages `zapier.com/apps/zendesk/integrations/productiveio` and `.../outline` (a slug 404 does not prove that no integration exists). The WebSearch budget ran out before searches for ServiceNow ticket exchange and Google Calendar.

1. **Copilot** (#18, #19, solution suggestions in #44): USD 50 per agent/month, paid yearly. It is an add-on for Suite Professional and is bundled in "Suite Enterprise + Copilot" (price not published). Source: [Pricing](https://www.zendesk.com/pricing/), [About Copilot](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/5524125586330-About-Zendesk-Copilot).

No allowance size or unit price is displayed. Source: [About AI agents](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/6970583409690-About-AI-agents).
3. **Zendesk Bridge** (#21): "During the EAP, Zendesk Bridge is available without charge. However, plan packaging and pricing might change". Source: [Bridge](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/11398710406554-Understanding-Zendesk-Bridge-and-allowing-external-AI-agents-to-perform-actions-in-Zendesk-EAP).

USD 50 per agent/month, paid yearly, as listed on the ES pricing page in the K.O. screen. Not re-checked on the CS page. Source: [Ticket deletion schedules](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/6388012977306-Creating-ticket-deletion-schedules), `analysis/ko_screen/zendesk.md`.
5. **Data Center Location** (#58): "free of charge for Zendesk Suite Professional or higher plans". Source: [DCL add-on](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408838409754-About-the-Data-Center-Location-add-on).
