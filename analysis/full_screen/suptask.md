# Suptask – full screen (requirements outside the K.O. set)
1. Products evaluated: **Suptask** (a Slack app plus a web app, app.suptask.com). Plans on https://www.suptask.com/pricing, per agent per month, billed annually / monthly: Starter $15 / $18, Professional $24 / $29, Growth $38 / $45, Custom "Contact Us". The page shows only "$" and does not name the currency. Trial: "all trials are 14 days with the Growth plan". The help center also mentions a "Free plan", which is not on the pricing page and was not evaluated. Plan levels below come from the per-plan check marks in the pricing comparison table, read from the page HTML (hidden icons excluded).

The help center (GitBook, support.suptask.com) was read through its official full-text export https://support.suptask.com/llms-full.txt (parts 1 and 2, all 160 articles). The article URLs cited below are the ones listed in https://support.suptask.com/llms.txt. **Blocked:** https://trust.suptask.com (Trust Center): "Vercel Security Checkpoint", status 429. Not circumvented. **404:** https://zapier.com/apps/suptask/integrations. **Conflicts between official pages** (noted per row): (a) the pricing page lists AI features from Professional or Growth, but the help center says "AI Assistants are a part of the Custom plan and available in beta"; (b) the Suptask MCP is "included in the new Professional plan and above" (MCP article, pricing page), but the AI overview says "AI MCP is a part of the Custom plan and available in alpha"; (c) the pricing page lists Webhooks from Professional, but the webhook guide says "Integrations is a part of the Custom plan. ## Add-on costs found
1. **AI features:** no add-on price. "AI features are available in all Suptask accounts. Every plan includes normal usage of these features at no additional charge." "Suptask does not publish fixed limits" and "may restrict, throttle or suspend AI features" if usage "materially exceeds normal usage" (https://www.suptask.com/trust/ai-policy, last edited 2026-06-13). Plan gating differs between the pricing page (Professional/Growth) and the help center (AI Assistants: Custom, beta).
2. **Integrations (managed service):** "Integrations is priced per configured integration on your account. When added on top of your monthly bill, the price per integration starts at $20 per month." They can also be "Embedded into the price of an Agent" (https://support.suptask.com/account-management/subscription-and-billing/integrations-billing). Many integration guides add: "Integrations is a part of the Custom plan. Additional cost might apply." This covers, for example, Google Sheets, GitHub and webhooks.
3. **Custom plan:** price on request ("Contact Us", "Volume Discounts", "Custom Contract Terms"). It is needed for AI Assistants (per the help center), the HubSpot and other CRM, HRIS and directory connectors, issue trackers, web forms, App Home customization, "Audit & Compliance", "HIPAA (BAA)" and "Custom Integrations" (https://www.suptask.com/pricing).
4. **SSO:** no separate SSO subscription found. "Single Sign-On" is part of Growth at $38 per agent per month billed annually ($45 monthly) (https://www.suptask.com/pricing).
5. **Third parties (prices not checked):** Tellspin (on-call partner app), Zapier or Make (optional), and the Slack plan (Suptask runs inside Slack; whether Slack Connect or Workflow Builder needs a paid Slack plan was not checked).
