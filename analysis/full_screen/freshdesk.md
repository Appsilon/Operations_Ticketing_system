## Add-on costs found
1. **Freddy AI Copilot** (#18, #19, #20): USD 29/agent/month, shown as the annual-billing price. The K.O. screen recorded USD 35 for monthly billing. It is offered as an add-on on Pro and Enterprise only, and can be bought for selected agents ("can be purchased and assigned to any number of agents of your choice"). Source: [Pricing](https://www.freshworks.com/freshdesk/pricing/), [Freddy add-ons](https://support.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/50000011515-manage-freddy-ai-add-ons).
2. **Freddy AI Agent sessions** (#30): a one-time 500 free sessions on Growth, Pro and Enterprise, then USD 49 per 100 sessions. An Email AI Agent session is a 72-hour window. Source: same two pages.
3. **MCP actions** (#21): 1,200 / 6,000 / 12,000 actions per year are included on Growth / Pro / Enterprise. Extra actions cost USD 15 per 1,000. Rate limits are 25 / 50 / 100 actions per minute. Source: [Pricing](https://www.freshworks.com/freshdesk/pricing/), [MCP integration](https://support.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/50000012670-model-context-protocol-mcp-integration-in-freshdesk-eap-).
4. **Connector app tasks** (#52, HubSpot Connector App; also the Azure AD connector): USD 80 per 5,000 tasks, listed on all three plans. Source: [Pricing](https://www.freshworks.com/freshdesk/pricing/), [HubSpot Connector](https://www.freshworks.com/apps/hubspot_connector_app_1/) ("IN APP PURCHASES").
5. **Day passes** for occasional agents: USD 2 / 7 / 12 per pass on Growth / Pro / Enterprise. Source: [Pricing](https://www.freshworks.com/freshdesk/pricing/).
6. **Additional API calls**: "An account can purchase additional API calls". The price is not published ("Contact us"). Source: [API reference](https://developers.freshdesk.com/api/).
7. **SSO**: no separate subscription. SAML SSO is included on Growth, Pro and Enterprise (pricing grid).
8. **Zapier** (#22, #43, #51, #55): Zapier's own subscription cost was not checked. The GitHub app (#35) is listed as FREE (third party). Workspaces are Enterprise only and "enabled on demand by Freshworks".
